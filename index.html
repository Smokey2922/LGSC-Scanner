What is a HWID and why is the license tied to it? ›
HWID (Hardware ID) is a unique fingerprint of your PC — generated from your CPU, motherboard, and drives. Tying your license to it means only you can activate and use it, preventing sharing or reselling.
Can someone clear their traces before being scanned? ›
BAM and some registry keys require admin rights to modify and persist across reboots, making them much harder to clear. LGSC also cross-references multiple sources, so clearing one rarely clears all.
Does it work on Windows 10? ›
Yes. LGSC Scanner is fully compatible with Windows 10 and Windows 11. Administrator privileges are required for full scan access.
